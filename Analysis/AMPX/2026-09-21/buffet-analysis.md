# Amprius Technologies (AMPX) — Buffett-Style Value Analysis — 2026-09-21

## Bottom line — Rating: D (Avoid)

Verdict: Amprius makes an understandable product with genuine technical progress — Q2 2026 revenue $34.03M (+126% YoY) at 27% gross margin, FY26 guide raised to revenue ≥$140M / margin ≥28% / net loss <$10M / adj. EBITDA ≥+$4M [SEC][2] — and a safe near-term balance sheet ($74.52M cash, $6.38M debt, $68.14M net cash, current ratio 7.17) [SEC][3][7]. But against Buffett buy conditions it fails where it matters: no durable wide moat (80+ patents pending/issued is real yet unproven at scale against CATL/Panasonic/Samsung SDI scale), no consistent 15%+ ROE/ROIC (TTM ROE -36.07%, ROIC -9.45%, WACC 17.05%) [7], no free cash flow (TTM OCF -$52.87M, FCF -$58.44M) [7], owner earnings deeply negative, continuous dilution (116.93M shares Dec 2024 → 146.08M Jun 2026) [SEC][3][4], high customer/supply concentration, and a price ($9.61, ~$1.40B mcap / ~$1.34B EV) [7] at ~12.9x sales and ~11.1x book on negative earnings with no 30–50% margin of safety to a conservatively discounted intrinsic value (weighted ~$4.7, see DCF). Operational momentum is real; value-investment qualification is not. Watch only — do not buy as a Buffett-style holding.

## 1. Business understanding & circle of competence

Amprius develops, manufactures and markets silicon-anode lithium-ion batteries (SiCore, SiMaxx) claiming up to ~2x energy density vs graphite, for aviation/drone/HAPS, defense and e-mobility [SEC][1][4]. Money is made selling finished battery products plus small customization/design services (FY25: $71.59M products + $0.33M services + $1.10M government grants = $73.01M) [SEC][4]. Production is asset-light: Fremont CA HQ + R&D + pilot line (expanding to 10 MWh, DIU-partially funded, ~40% tools installing in Q2) plus contract manufacturing via Berzelius (Nanjing) and the Korea Battery Alliance (>2.0 GWh annual SiCore access, pouch/cylindrical/prismatic) [SEC][2][3]. Commercial proof points in Q2–Q3 2026: $24.0M European drone SiCore cylindrical order, multi-year Stark Future e-motorcycle agreement (>$100M opportunity from 2027), Redwire Stalker Block 30 cells, SiCore500 500 Wh/kg launch (commercial target Q4 2026) [SEC][2][5].

Simple enough to describe, but not simple to predict — the classic Buffett distinction. Whether silicon-anode cells hold cycle life/safety/cost at volume, pass long aviation/defense qualification, diversify from a few drone/defense buyers, and survive NDAA/China-export-control and tariff shocks is outside an ordinary circle of predictable, repeat-purchase economics [SEC][3][4]. Batteries will be needed in 10 years — yes. That Amprius will be the scaled, high-return survivor — unknowable today. Verdict: marginal circle pass on product, fail on predictability.

## 2. Economic moat analysis

**Intangible assets — weak/narrow.** 80+ patents issued or pending at Dec 31 2025 plus 15 years of silicon-anode know-how/trade secrets [SEC][4]; SiCore to 450 Wh/kg / up to 1,400 cycles (lab/platform claims) and SiCore500 at 500 Wh/kg on conventional equipment [SEC][4][5]. Real, but no consumer brand premium, short patent-life visibility disclosed, and incumbents (CATL, Panasonic, Samsung SDI) plus funded startups hold far larger portfolios and scale. Design wins (AALTO/Airbus Zephyr 67-day flight, AeroVironment, BAE, Teledyne FLIR, Nokia Drone Networks) validate performance [SEC][4][5] but do not confer pricing power yet — gross margin, while improving (FY24 -76% → FY25 +11% → TTM 22.25% → Q2 27%) [7][SEC][2][4], is still subscale and customer-concentrated.

**Switching costs — moderate in niches, narrow overall.** Aviation/defense qualification and custom formats create stickiness once designed in (HAPS fixed-wing/sUAS pouch SAE JA1016, Stalker ISR) [SEC][5]. But concentration cuts both ways: Q2 2026 two customers 25% of revenue (vs 52% prior year); H1 three customers 39% (vs 59%); AR two customers ~54%; FY25 one customer $27.1M (~37% of revenue); Ukraine shipments $20.5M H1 / $33.0M FY25 [SEC][3][4]. Losing one program hurts more than any switching moat helps. No churn/retention series disclosed.

**Network effects — none.** More users do not make the battery more valuable; no two-sided platform. Pack-partner additions help distribution, not network compounding [SEC][2].

**Cost advantages — none demonstrated.** No scale economy yet: TTM revenue $109.23M vs giants' billions; 97 full-time + 12 contractors (27 R&D, 45 manufacturing) [SEC][4][7]. Contract manufacturing avoids capex (TTM capex $5.57M, FY26 guide <$10M) [7][SEC][2] but cedes process control and leaves Berzelius dependency (exclusive US/Canada/Mexico silicon-anode material rights) plus DIU/NDAA sourcing constraints [SEC][3][4]. No location/resource advantage; Fremont pilot is capability, not low-cost scale.

**Moat rating: Narrow at best (emerging technical edge; no wide moat).** Fails the Buffett wide-and-sustainable test.

## 3. Management quality

**Integrity & shareholder orientation — mixed, no red-flag fraud.** Disclosures are candid on pain points: $19.1M Brighton 774k-sq-ft lease/CIP impairment, January 2026 Brighton termination (-$20.90M operating-lease-liability outflow in H1 OCF), $1.921M warrant-modification charge, bill-and-hold $15.7M, Ukraine/geopolitical and China-export-control exposures [SEC][3][4]. Team is new: CEO Tom Stepien (director since May 2025; ex-South 8, Primus Power, Applied Materials), CFO Ricardo Rodriguez (since Oct 2025; ex-Aspen Aerogels, Aptiv, Lazard), CTO Dr. Ionel Stefan (since 2015; long-tenured technologist) [11]. Short shared track record means no 3-year shareholder-letter record to grade. Alignment is weak: insider ownership only 2.19% vs institutions 74.30% [7]; August 2026 Form 4 + 144 cluster shows insider selling activity, not open-market buying [12]; SBC is material ($7.41M FY25 ≈ 10% of revenue; H1 2026 $4.60M; FY26 guide $8.30M ≈ 6% of guided revenue) [SEC][2][4]. Proxy/DEF 14A detail was not separately fetched (SEC direct browse blocked during research); compensation judged from 10-K/10-Q SBC, insider %, and filing activity — a limitation.

**Capital allocation — poor history, recently more disciplined.** ATM dilution funded losses: ~10.72M shares / $63.58M net FY25; H1 2025 ~$18.20M ATM; H1 2026 $14.18M warrant exercises + $11.22M option exercises; shares 116.93M → 134.54M → 146.08M [SEC][3][4][7]. Brighton mega-lease impaired — a value-destroying expansion reversed. Pivot to contract manufacturing + small Fremont pilot is the right asset-light correction, and DIU $18.1M award offsets some R&D (other income $1.5M H1) [SEC][2][3]. No dividends/buybacks (correct while loss-making); FCF use is survival + WC, not shareholder return. No counter-cyclical investing evidence; no M&A. Grade: below Buffett standard.

**Operational capability — improving but not leading.** Gross margin trajectory is the bright spot (see §4), and Q2 adj. EBITDA -$1.01M improved 53% YoY toward FY26 ≥+$4M target [SEC][2]. But ROE -36.07%, ROA -8.55%, ROIC -9.45% vs WACC 17.05% [7]; peers also loss-making (ENVX gross 18.83%, net -$170M TTM; SES gross 27.75%, net -$68M; QS pre-revenue net -$405M) [8][9][10] — Amprius is relatively better on sales scale ($109M TTM vs ENVX $35.9M, SES $23.5M) but not a profitability leader. No pricing-power evidence.

## 4. Financial health & earnings quality

**Profitability (trend, not 10-year — company has no 10-year public profit record).** FY24 revenue $24.17M → FY25 $73.01M (+202%); gross -$18.33M → +$8.26M; net -$44.67M → -$44.02M; EPS -$0.45 → -$0.35 [SEC][4]. ROE never >15%, relies on no leverage benefit (D/E 0.05); ROIC < WACC every period [7]. Earnings now come from core battery sales (not one-offs), but flattered by $1.1M FY25 grants and $1.5M H1 DIU other income [SEC][2][4].

**Cash-flow quality — poor.** TTM OCF -$52.87M, capex -$5.57M, FCF -$58.44M (-$0.40/sh) [7]. H1 2026 OCF -$40.13M includes -$20.90M Brighton lease-termination outflow; AR +$16.84M and inventories +$4.77M consumed cash as sales ramped (AR $40.65M, inv $11.51M at Jun 30) [SEC][2][3]. OCF/net income >1 test is meaningless while loss-making. CapEx/revenue is low (~5% TTM) thanks to outsourcing — a plus — but WC intensity replaces it as the cash drain. Receivables allowance $0.3M; no major bad-debt history disclosed [SEC][3].

**Financial safety — strong today, finite runway.** Cash $74.52M, debt $6.38M, net cash $68.14M ($0.47/sh), equity $125.64M ($0.86/sh), current 7.17 / quick 6.28, D/E 0.05, Altman Z 41.61, Piotroski 3 [7][SEC][3]. 10-Q states cash funds ~12 months from issuance; warrants/options added $25.4M H1 financing [SEC][3]. No hidden-debt signal beyond leases ($6.38M total lease liabilities at Jun 30 post-termination vs $39.87M at Dec 31) [SEC][3]. Safety is real but depends on hitting FY26 targets or raising again — shares +17.21% YoY already [7].

**Accounting prudence — no aggressive signal found; watch items noted.** No policy-change flag; EGC status limits comparability [SEC][3]. Bill-and-hold ($15.7M FY25 vs $4.9M FY24) and grant/other-income classification merit skepticism but are disclosed [SEC][4]. Non-GAAP adj. EBITDA adds back SBC, Brighton costs, warrant mod and interest — Buffett analysis uses GAAP/owner earnings, where the business still loses money.

## 5. Long-term growth prospects

**Industry — growing but brutal.** Silicon-anode / high-energy lithium-ion for drones, HAPS, defense and e-mobility should grow over 10 years; structure is fragmented-to-oligopolistic (CATL/Panasonic/Samsung SDI at scale + ENVX/QS/SES/Solid Power startups) with constant disruption risk (solid-state, lithium-metal, sodium-ion) [8][9][10]. Tariffs, China battery/material export controls (suspended to at least Nov 2026 talks), and NDAA compliance add cost/supply volatility [SEC][3].

**Competitive position — tiny, technically differentiated, commercially early.** Share trend is up from a tiny base (analyst 2026 $140.65M / 2027 $212.11M, +50.8%) [6]; advantages are Wh/kg and aviation qualification, not scale, cost or distribution. No demonstrated ability to raise price without losing sockets. Stark >$100M from 2027 and $24M drone order are pipeline, not backlog-cash; SiCore500 must still clear customer qualification and Fremont-to-Korea scale-up in Q4 2026 [SEC][2][5].

**Sustainable growth — organic but capital-hungry in WC terms.** Growth to date is organic (no M&A engine). Second curve exists on paper (SiCore500, Stark, Redwire, DIU) but each requires qualification, working capital and partner execution. History says growth consumes equity (see §3 dilution). Verdict: high uncertainty — fails Buffett clarity test.

Downside if execution slips: beta 2.34, 15.9% short interest, price already -29% vs 100/200-day SMAs and -18% over 52 weeks [7] — technical weakness offers no value cushion. ## 9. Risks

- **Industry/macro:** battery price wars and solid-state/lithium-metal substitution; tariffs, China export controls, NDAA sourcing; defense-budget and Ukraine-demand volatility ($20.5M H1 Ukraine) [SEC][3][4].
- **Company-specific:** customer concentration (top-2/3 = 25–39% sales; 54% AR) [SEC][3]; Berzelius + Korea Alliance supply/quality dependence; >2 GWh access is access, not owned output [SEC][3]; SiCore500 qualification/volume risk (Q4 2026 target) [5]; Stark >$100M timing/ramp risk from 2027 [SEC][2]; key-person (CTO Stefan) and new CEO/CFO execution risk [11]; Brighton-style expansion missteps repeating.
- **Financial:** persistent burn (FCF -$58.4M TTM) and WC absorption force future raises; warrants/options/ATM overhang (dilution 17.2% YoY) [7][SEC][3][4]; Form 4/144 selling pressure Aug 2026 [12].
- **Valuation:** 12.9x sales / 11.1x book on losses already embeds optimism; any guide cut (FY26 ≥$140M / ≥28% / <$10M loss / +$4M EBITDA [SEC][2]) triggers severe compression given beta/shorts.

## 10. Investment-decision framework & rating

Buy requires ALL: simple/predictable ✓/✗ (product yes, economics no) · wide moat ✗ · integrity+allocation ✗ (mixed/new, dilution, impaired lease) · ample FCF ✗ · clear growth ✗ (possible, not clear) · ≥30% MOS ✗ · controllable risks ✗. Hold requires solid moat, no integrity issue, not severely overvalued — overvaluation is severe. Sell triggers (overvaluation >50% above IV on neutral, better opportunities, thesis unproven) point to avoid.

**Final rating: D (Avoid).** Material progress deserves monitoring — raise to C (Watch) only on: GAAP profitability with OCF > net income, FCF positive two consecutive quarters without WC games, top-3 concentration <25% with Stark converting to delivered revenue, insider buying (not 144 sales), and price offering ≥30% discount to a neutral DCF (~≤$2.5–$3.5). Until then, a Buffett buyer passes.
