03The Debate: Agreement, Contention, Resolution

Round 1: opening statements. Round 2: uncontested common ground. Round 3: six disputed points, cross-examined to resolution. Round 4: final concessions.

Uncontested consensus core

The inflection is real. Record $34.03M Q2 (+126% YoY) at 27% gross margin, TTM $109.23M, FY26 guide raised twice (≥$140M / ≥28% / adj. EBITDA ≥+$4M) — revenue and margin momentum are facts, not narrative.
No current owner earnings to buy. Owner earnings ~−$60M, FCF −$58.44M TTM, OCF −$52.87M, ROE −36.07%, ROIC −9.45%. The investment is entirely a wager on future per-share cash flow.
The moat is narrow at best. Emerging silicon-anode/qualification edge; no scale, cost, network, or pricing-power moat against CATL/Panasonic/Samsung SDI. Nobody grants wide or even moderate.
Dilution is the per-share tax. Shares 116.93M → 146.08M in eighteen months (+17.2% YoY), ~22M of warrant/option/RSU overhang (~15%), SBC ~$8.3M guide. Enterprise value can grow while per-share value stalls.
Double concentration fragility. Two customers ~54% of AR ($40.65M) with Ukraine/EMEA weight, stacked on Berzelius + Korea contract-manufacturer dependence plus tariff/China-control exposure.
Solvent, not strong. Cash $74.52M vs debt $6.38M (net cash $68.14M); current ratio 7.17. No near-term distress — but roughly a year of runway absent inflection or financing.
$9.61 embeds successful execution. ~12.9x sales and ~11.1x book on negative earnings; every intrinsic-value model sits below the price; margin of safety is absent to negative.
One valuation-critical variable: forward ROIC vs the cost of capital (via terminal margin and WACC). WACC and terminal margin move value 2–3x more than revenue headlines; all five lenses reduce to the same hinge.

Contested points — and how they were resolved

Reference price: one price, two objects. Buffett concedes disclosure quality is genuinely good; Damodaran concedes execution positives. Resolved: consensus 5/10 — candid operators, unproven allocators.
Is positive 12-month price expectancy a reason to buy? Druckenmiller +27% and Soros +49–53% (to ~$14.30) are tail-driven (10–20% weights on +87–233% outcomes) with uninsurable left tails; both concede expectancy without timing is not a position. Resolved: positive expectancy justifies monitoring with staged, stop-defined speculative capital only — and is why nobody rates E/Short.

07What Changes the Rating

Upgrade D → C/B when:
  1. Forward ROIC demonstrably crossing and holding above WACC — realized gross ≥28% with CM yields at spec
  2. FCF inflection: sustainably positive FCF per diluted share for two consecutive quarters
  3. Dilution pause: share count stabilizes; insiders buy openly (not 144 sales); SBC as % of revenue declines
  4. Diversification: top-3 concentration falling with Stark converting to delivered, prepaid revenue from 2027
  5. A margin of safety returning — price into $7.50–$9.75 with Q3 confirmation (C), or into $2.00–$4.00 on fundamentals (B)
Downgrade D → E if:
  1. Q3 miss plus FY26 guide cut (revenue, margin, or adj. EBITDA) — the priced-in heroics evaporate
  2. Dilutive raise at weak prices (>10% in any 12 months) cementing the negative financing loop
  3. Loss or pause of an anchor customer (EMEA/Ukraine) or SiCore500 qualification slip past Q4 2026
  4. CM yield/cost failure or China export-control enforcement strangling the Berzelius/Korea chain
  5. Terminal-margin commoditization — gross retreating toward the teens on competition

09The Five Consensus Conclusions

The Business. Amprius is a real silicon-anode producer with aviation-led traction — record $34.03M quarters at 27% gross margin, a $24.0M drone order, a >$100M Stark opportunity from 2027, and SiCore500 on conventional kit — scaling asset-light through Korean partners with DIU backing. It is a pre-profit scale-up, not a franchise.
The Economics. The sole determinant of long-term per-share value is forward ROIC vs the cost of capital, driven by terminal margin and WACC — not revenue headlines. History (−9.45% ROIC, −53.5% FCF margin) destroys value; the Base assumes a thin +1.5pp forward spread (15% vs 13.5%) that is plausible but entirely unproven.
The Market's Expectations. At $9.61 the market prices ~$1.0–1.5B of mature revenue at ~20–25% EBIT margins — the Bull-to-Extreme path (35% combined probability) treated as the median. Aggressive verging on heroic; the unanimous-analyst $23 target (+139%) is the sentiment anchor for that pricing.
The Valuation. Consensus base intrinsic value ~$2.52/share; probability-weighted ~$4.26/share; bear ~$0.46–1.00; bull ~$6.87 with an extreme tail ~$14.15. Damodaran workbook: $0.46 / $0.93 / $2.36 / $5.85 / $14.15, weighted $3.93. Confidence: Low/Medium. Expected upside ≈ −56%, 5-year annualized ≈ −15.0%.
The Investment Decision. D — AVOID / DO NOT INITIATE at $9.61 (do-not-initiate band $9.75 and above). Only small (1–3%) speculative capital stages $7.50–$9.75 with Q3 confirmation; fundamental capital begins accumulating at $2.00–$4.00, with high conviction below $2.00 — provided the monitoring hierarchy (rates/margins > ROIC > FCF-per-share > order conversion > dilution) stays intact.

10Final Consensus Judgment

“

At a price of $9.61, the market is effectively paying for Amprius to execute its Bull-to-Extreme scale-up — roughly $1.0–1.5B of mature revenue at ~20–25% EBIT margins with smooth contract-manufacturing yields, on-time SiCore500 qualification, Stark converting in full, and no further per-share dilution — with essentially no penalty for concentration, qualification risk, or the Stage 4 tape. Five independent frameworks — owner-earnings value, multidisciplinary quality, fundamental DCF, macro/liquidity-driven expectations, and reflexivity — all refuse investment-sized exposure at this price, all land on a D (Avoid / Do not initiate), and all place the valuation-critical variable on the same hinge: whether forward ROIC exceeds the ~13.5% cost of capital. We agree the technology is genuinely impressive, the revenue/margin inflection is factual, dilution-plus-concentration is the certain left friction, and $9.61 compensates neither. The most dangerous risks are a dilutive raise into weakness cementing the negative loop, an anchor-customer or SiCore500 qualification failure, CM/yield or China-supply disruption, and terminal margins compressing toward commodity levels. Therefore, at today's price the consensus is to wait: allow only staged 1–3% speculation on weakness inside $7.50–$9.75 with Q3 confirmation, accumulate investment capital at $2.00–$4.00, demand high-conviction pricing below $2.00, and let demonstrated return on capital — not drone headlines or the $23 analyst target — decide whether this impressive technology becomes a good investment.
