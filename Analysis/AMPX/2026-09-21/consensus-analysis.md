# Part II — The Consensus Synthesis

## 1. The Business (What You Actually Own)

Amprius designs silicon-anode lithium-ion cells (SiCore, SiMaxx, now SiCore500) delivering up to ~2x graphite energy density, sells them into weight-decides-everything niches — long-endurance drones, HAPS, aerospace/defense, performance e-mobility — and manufactures asset-light via its Fremont pilot plus Korean contract-manufacturing partners, with DIU co-funding part of the expansion. FY25 was 98% finished battery product ($71.59M of $73.01M); the commercial proof points are the $24M European drone order, the Stark Future >$100M multi-year opportunity from 2027, Redwire's Stalker Block 30, and SiCore500's Q4 2026 commercial target.

**The consensus story:**

> **Amprius can create substantial value if it converts a genuine Wh/kg lead into diversified, repeat, high-yield volume through contract manufacturers faster than dilution, concentration, and commoditization eat the per-share economics — and if the returns on that growth exceed its cost of capital. The technology is plausible. The per-share economics are not yet proven.**

## 2. The Economics (The One Variable That Matters)

Every profile, through different vocabularies, reaches the same place:

$$ \text{Value is created only if} \quad \text{Forward ROIC} > \text{Cost of Capital} \quad (\approx 13.5\% \text{ Damodaran blend; } 17.05\% \text{ quant ref}) $$

- History destroys value: ROIC −9.45% vs any cost of capital, FCF margin −53.5%.
- The Base case assumes the sign flip: 15% forward ROIC vs 13.5% WACC — a thin +1.5pp spread that carries the entire $2.36.
- Damodaran's sensitivity ranking is uncontested: **WACC ≈ terminal margin > terminal growth > Y1 growth > ROIC level** — revenue headlines move IV ~10–12% where a 1pp WACC or 5pp terminal-margin move swings it 30–45%.

**Consensus monitoring hierarchy:** `WACC/beta regime > realized gross & terminal margin > incremental ROIC > FCF per diluted share > order conversion ($24M EU / Stark / SiCore500 qualification) > diluted share count`.

## 3. Management

Candid operators with two straight guide raises, real margin delivery, marquee wins, and an honestly-unwound Brighton mistake corrected into the right asset-light model — set against a value-destroying expansion history, 17%-a-year dilution, ~22M of overhang, thin insider ownership, and growth that has never earned its cost of capital. **5/10.**
