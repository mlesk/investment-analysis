## 1. Start With the Macro, Not the Company

Dominant force right now: **restrictive re-acceleration — inflation + hawkish Fed + rising long yields**, not earnings.

- **Growth:** Fed Sep 16: "expanding at solid pace," productivity strong, capex robust, jobs keeping pace, unemployment little changed [2]. Trading Economics notes manufacturing output soft but capacity utilization at 1‑yr high [5]. Solid growth = Fed has cover to stay tight.
- **Inflation:** Aug CPI 3.4% YY (flat vs Jul, in-line), +0.4% MoM (most in 3 months); gasoline +27.4% YY drove >1/3 of monthly gain; shelter 3.0%, food 2.7%; core CPI +0.3% MoM (hotter than 0.2% expected), 2.4% YY (lowest since Mar 2021) [5]. PPI 5.4% YY [5]. Fed: "inflation remains elevated" [2]. Energy shock from Iran war easing but oil still ~$92 keeps upside risk [3][5].
- **Monetary:** FOMC Sep 16 **hiked +25bp to 3.75–4.00%**, unanimous 12–0, "timelier return to 2%" [2]. This is the opposite of the rate-cut tailwind high-beta growth needs. Real rates high with 10Y ~4.96% [3].
- **Fiscal / industrial:** Defense/drone spend is the offsetting tailwind: DIU pushing commercial-to-combat conversion and Bridge program [9]; AMPX Fremont expansion partially DIU-funded [1]; company cites government awards/policies as forward driver but also flags tariffs, China regulatory risk, trade barriers on partners/suppliers [1].
- **Global:** Dollar index ~100.4 (firm), gold $4,381 (elevated — risk-hedge bid), oil $92, BTC $86.5K [3]. Strong dollar + high oil + high gold = late-cycle / geopolitical-risk mix, bad for unprofitable duration assets.

> What is the dominant macro force? **Tightening financial conditions via higher-for-longer rates.** Likely to strengthen/weaken over 6–18mo? My estimate: **stay tight near-term, ease only if growth or oil rolls over — headwind for AMPX multiple for at least 1–2 quarters.**
