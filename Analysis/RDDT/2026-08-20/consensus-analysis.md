# Part II — The Consensus Synthesis

## 1. The Business (What You Actually Own)

Reddit is the world's largest network of interest-based discussion communities: 130.3M DAUq (+18%), 514.6M WAUq (+24%), 100k+ active communities, and 26+ billion posts and comments generated free by users across two decades. It monetizes through advertising ($2,625M TTM, 94% of revenue), data licensing to AI labs ($153.5M TTM), and early optionality in e-commerce (Shopify GA), video, and international (84% revenue growth). The flywheel — more users → more conversation → better search visibility and advertiser value → more revenue — is genuine and two-sided.

**The consensus story:**

> **Reddit can be worth multiples of today's price if the community moat keeps converting into ARPU compounding while the Google referral pathway holds — and worth a small fraction of it if AI search re-platforms that pathway. The business is not the question. The pathway is.**

## 2. The Economics (The One Variable That Matters)

Every profile, through different vocabularies, reaches the same place:

$$ \text{Value is preserved only if} \quad \text{Google-sourced traffic (}\sim\!50\%\text{) stays a pathway, not a wall} $$

- If AI Overviews and algorithm changes prove benign, DAUq keeps compounding, ARPU (+36%) grinds toward Meta levels, and the ~$60M/yr Google licensing deal renews → **the base case ($109.50 workbook) is conservative** and the bull tail is real.
- If referrals decay structurally — the template being the Q2'26 DAUq miss and the −21% print — DAU growth stalls, ARPU can't offset, and the bear/conservative outcomes ($45.27 / $70.40, 35% combined) arrive.
- The answer is unknowable today, which is precisely why the stock is a debate rather than an obvious buy or short — and why a 12% WACC (Damodaran) rather than the market's implied ~9.9% is the defensible discount.

**Consensus monitoring hierarchy:** `Google referral health → DAUq (US logged-in) → ARPU compounding → data-licensing renewal → SBC % revenue → Q3'26 print`.

## 3. Management

Founder-led, candid, restrained on cash pay ($3.1M CEO comp, 11.3:1 ratio), disciplined on the buyback — but a controlled company (~74% Huffman voting power), an SBC bill of ~39% of net income, a buyback executing above intrinsic value, and a disclosure retreat on logged-in DAUq "right as the metric turns." **6/10.**
