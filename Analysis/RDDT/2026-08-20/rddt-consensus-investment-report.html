03The Debate: Agreement, Contention, Resolution

Round 1: opening statements. Round 2: uncontested common ground. Round 3: six disputed points, cross-examined to resolution. Round 4: final concessions. Minervini's trading view is recorded but stands outside the valuation consensus.

Uncontested consensus core

The franchise is exceptional. 91.4% gross margin, 28.25% operating margin, 36.7% FCF margin, ~$7.5M TTM capex, $2.77B net cash, 8 straight quarters of 60%+ revenue growth.
The moat is wide on the community side, rented on the distribution side. The 20-year, 26B+ post corpus and two-sided network effect are genuinely defensible; ~50% of traffic arriving via Google is not.
SBC is the per-share tax. $338M TTM = 12.2% of revenue and ~39% of net income; how each lens treats it is the single largest driver of the valuation spread.
The Q2'26 print was a template event, not an operating collapse. −21% on 2026-07-30 while revenue grew +61.1% — the market repriced the Google referral pathway, not the P&L.
No margin of safety at $151.71. Five of six lenses place their central value below the price; nobody calls the stock cheap.
The S&P 500 inclusion was a flow event, not a story change. +16.5% on 08-14 fully faded by 08-19; index membership does not change the Google dependency.
Google is a four-way dependency — traffic source (~50%), advertising rival, AI-disintermediator (AI Overviews), and licensing customer (~$60M/yr, renewal in doubt).
Unanimous: do not initiate at $151.71. C (Buffett), C (Munger), C (Damodaran), C+ (Druckenmiller), B− (Soros), D-trading (Minervini). No outright buyer today; no one shorts the moat.
One valuation-critical variable: the durability of Google-sourced traffic. Every lens, in its own vocabulary, lands on the same hinge.
The distribution is wide and two-tailed. Beta 2.03; bear cases −70% to −85%; bull tails +78% to +179%; a binary licensing renewal; a crowded short base.

09The Five Consensus Conclusions

The Business. Reddit is one of the great consumer-internet franchises — “the toll booth on human conversation” — a wide-moat community network with 91.4% gross margins, 36.7% FCF margins, near-zero capital intensity, a $2.77B net-cash fortress, and a 26B+ post corpus that search engines and AI labs pay to license. The business quality is not in dispute.
The Economics. The sole determinant of long-term value is the durability of Google-sourced traffic. If the referral pathway holds, ARPU compounding (+36%) makes the base case conservative; if AI search re-platforms it, the bear/conservative outcomes ($45.27 / $70.40 workbook, 35% combined) arrive. Monitoring hierarchy: Google referral health → DAUq (US logged-in) → ARPU → data-licensing renewal → SBC % revenue → Q3'26 print.
The Market's Expectations. At $151.71 the market assumes ~53% first-year revenue growth at a 12% WACC (Damodaran's reverse DCF) — bull-case execution priced as the base expectation, ~26% above consensus revenue. The $215.63 average PT is anchored to forward non-GAAP EPS (~$7.56) that ignores normalized taxes and the SBC bill. Aggressive, not impossible — and no bargain.
The Valuation. Consensus base intrinsic value ~$100/share; probability-weighted ~$124/share; bear ~$50; bull ~$290 with a workbook extreme of $423.27. Damodaran's verified workbook: $45.27 / $70.40 / $109.50 / $194.16 / $423.27, weighted $141.61. Confidence: Low/Medium. Both the consensus base and the consensus weighted value are below the $151.71 price — expected upside ≈ −19%, 5-year annualized ≈ −4.0%.
The Investment Decision. C — HOLD / WATCH at $151.71; do not initiate at or above ~$150. Begin accumulating at $95–115, build at $75–95, full conviction under $75 (Munger's prices below $45), provided the monitoring hierarchy stays intact. Upgrades require proof — the Q3'26 print, the Google renewal, and ARPU durability, not the stock price, will deliver the verdict.

10Final Consensus Judgment

“

At a price of $151.71, the market is effectively paying for Reddit to execute its optimistic scenario — ~53% first-year growth, sustained ARPU compounding, and a benign Google relationship — with essentially no penalty for the ~$338M/yr SBC bill, the binary licensing renewal, or the controlled-company governance. Six independent frameworks — owner-earnings value, multidisciplinary quality, fundamental DCF, macro/liquidity expectations, reflexivity, and trend trading — all refuse to buy at this price. Five land on a C (Hold/Watch) or better-but-conditional (Druckenmiller C+, Soros B−), and one reads the chart as an outright D. We agree the franchise is exceptional, the moat is wide on the community side and rented on the distribution side, SBC is the per-share tax, management is excellent-but-scored-6/10 for the disclosure retreat, and the valuation already pays for the optimistic path. The most dangerous risks are a structural Google-referral decline, a terminated data-licensing deal, an SBC re-acceleration, and a de-rating spiral on the first further deceleration. Therefore, at today's price the consensus is to hold and wait: do not initiate at or above ~$150, accumulate in stages at $95–115 and $75–95, buy with conviction under $75, and let the Q3'26 print, the Google renewal, and the ARPU trajectory — not the AI-victim narrative or the index membership — decide whether this wonderful company becomes a wonderful investment.
