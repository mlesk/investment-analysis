# Reddit, Inc. (RDDT) — Damodaran-Lens Valuation Analysis

**Analysis date:** 2026-08-20 · **Reference price:** $151.71 (2026-08-19 close) · **Market cap:** $29.19B · **Shares:** 192.4M basic / 202.0M diluted

---

## Bottom Line

**Rating: C — Hold/watch at $151.71.** Reddit is a genuinely exceptional business in the middle of a textbook monetization arc — 61% revenue growth, 28.3% operating margins, $1.02B of free cash flow, and a fortress balance sheet with $2.77B of net cash. But valuation is about **cash flows, growth, and risk** — not business quality. My probability-weighted intrinsic value is **$141.61/share** (base case $109.50, diluted $134.88) — about **7% below the price** — and the base case is **28% below** it, so there is no margin of safety at $151.71. The reverse-DCF check is decisive: at a 12.0% cost of capital, today's price embeds ~53% revenue growth next year — i.e., revenue ~26% above the FY2026 consensus of $3.38B — followed by 48%/43%/38%/33% growth and operating margins reaching 34%. That is an optimistic-scenario trajectory, and it must be achieved while ~50% of Reddit's traffic depends on Google, whose algorithm changes and AI Overviews are already eroding referral traffic (the Q2'26 DAUq miss that cost the stock 21%). Reddit deserves a premium multiple; it does not deserve a price that pays for the optimistic path as the expected one. I would be a buyer in the $75–95 zone, not at $151.71.

## 1. Business Understanding & Fundamental Story

**What Reddit does, simply:** Reddit runs ~100k+ online communities ("subreddits") where users post text, links, and now increasingly audio/video. It sells **advertising** on those communities (TTM $2,625M, 94.5% of revenue) and licenses **data** (TTM $153.5M "other," which includes AI-training/licensing deals) [SA][4]. Advertisers pay because Reddit offers intent-rich, context-specific attention at a large scale; data customers pay because 20 years of human conversation is premium AI-training material.

**Unit economics & economic structure:** Cost of revenue is ~9% of sales (gross margin 91.4%) because users create the content for free — Reddit pays for infrastructure and content moderation, not content. Operating expenses are people-heavy (R&D $228M and S&M $196M in Q2'26 alone) and scale slower than revenue, which is why the operating margin has expanded from −43.1% (FY2024) to +28.3% (TTM) [SA][4][13]. Capital intensity is near zero: TTM capex is just **$7.5M** (0.3% of revenue) and D&A $16.5M [SA][3]. Working capital is favorable (receivables + inventory light). This is a **platform network business**: the marginal dollar of revenue costs cents of capital — the opposite of Meta's $89B capex problem.

**Fundamental story:** *"Reddit will create value because its community network and conversational corpus are scarce, its advertising is still priced below its engagement share, and its asset-light model converts revenue to cash at a 37% FCF margin while revenue grows ~30%+."* 

| Driver | Today | Expected change | Evidence | What breaks it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cash flows | $1,019M FCF (36.7% margin) | Margin → 30–34% by Y5 | Op margin 28.3% and climbing; SBC normalizing | SBC re-acceleration; AI moderation costs |
| Growth | +66.6% TTM, +61.1% Q2'26 | Decay to ~15% by Y5 | Consensus +53%/'26, +31%/'27 [SA][5] | ~50% of traffic is Google-sourced; DAUq already missed Q2'26 [12] |
| Risk | Beta 2.03; no debt | Beta ↓ as business matures | Net cash; FCF positive | Google dependence, AI search displacement |

**Lifecycle:** Growth-stage platform (IPO Mar 2024, first profitable years 2025–26). Model: **three-stage growth** approximated by a 5-year explicit forecast with decaying growth to a stable-growth terminal.

## 2. Historical Fundamental Analysis

Revenue (fiscal years): 2021 $484.9M → 2022 $666.7M → 2023 $804.0M → 2024 $1,300.2M → 2025 $2,202.5M → TTM $2,778.8M [SA][4]. Growth accelerated from ~20% (2023) to 62–69% in 2024–26 as (a) the ad stack matured, (b) Google's 2023–24 algorithm shift and search deals funneled massive logged-out traffic, and (c) AI data licensing began. Revenue is essentially **all organic** — no meaningful M&A (goodwill just $42.2M) [SEC][1]. US is 80% of revenue ($2.23B TTM), rest-of-world $552M and growing faster [SA][13]. Revenue is ~95% advertising — recurring-ish but **cyclical with the digital-ad market**, and concentrated in Google-referred traffic.

**Profitability:** Gross margin stable at 91.4%. Operating margin: −43.1% (2024) → +20.1% (2025) → +28.3% TTM [SA][4] — classic operating leverage. Q2'26 op income was $231.7M on $804.9M revenue (28.8%), Q1'26 27.6% [SA][13]. ROE 30.7%; stockanalysis-computed ROIC 163.9% — distorted by tiny invested capital and a ~1% cash tax rate; see normalization in §5.

**Capital intensity:** CapEx $7.5M TTM vs $16.5M D&A — Reddit is one of the least capital-hungry public companies. Reinvestment is almost entirely **operating expense (hiring, SBC)**, which is why accounting margins understate steady-state economics but also why SBC is the real economic cost.

**Accounting vs economic earnings:** Reported TTM net income $871.1M benefits from a ~0.8% effective tax rate (NOLs). Economic earnings are lower on taxes (normalized ~24% on ~$785M EBIT = −$180M) and on SBC ($338M TTM, only partly offset by the cash-tax shield). **Economically-adjusted earnings ≈ $550–600M**, against $1,019M reported FCF — see §5.

## 3. Competitive Advantage & Industry Economics

**Industry:** Global digital advertising (~$800B+, growing high-single-digits) and the emerging **AI-training-data market**. Competition: Meta, Google/YouTube, TikTok, Pinterest, Snap for ad dollars; every major LLM lab for corpus access. Reddit's niche — **high-intent, text-first, community-authentic** — is structurally different from Meta/TikTok's entertainment feeds; brand advertisers use Reddit for consideration/research moments.

**Moat mechanics (Damodaran test: does it show up in margins/returns/durability?):**
- **Network effects / user-generated content:** strong, two-sided (more communities → more content → more search relevance → more users). Shows up in zero-content-cost 91% gross margins.
- **Data advantage:** 20-year conversational corpus; licensing monopoly on its own data; defensible via technical + legal walls (litigating scrapers). Shows up in the "other" revenue line — but small today ($153.5M TTM).
- **Switching costs:** low for users (subreddits can be cloned), **high for advertisers' performance data** and for AI labs needing fresh, real human conversation.
- **Key vulnerability:** distribution is **not owned**. ~50% of traffic arrives via Google [12], and Google's AI Overviews + algorithm changes have already caused a Q2'26 DAUq miss and a 21% single-day wipeout [12][1]. When your moat depends on a competitor's search results page, it is a **partial moat with a giant easement**.

**Excess returns:** On economic capital (~$0.5–0.7B ex-cash), ROIC is triple-digit; on a normalized basis I use 90% initial declining to 30% terminal. The spread ROIC − WACC is enormous **today**; the question is duration. Google dependence and AI disruption argue for fast decay in the tail scenarios.

**Competitive Advantage Rating: Strong** (network + data), with a **Weak** floor if Google-owned distribution turns hostile.

## 4. Management & Capital Allocation

Observable record (what Damodaran actually grades):
- **Internal reinvestment:** excellent — near-zero capex, high-ROIC growth. Op margin expanded ~71pts in two years [SA][4].
- **Acquisitions:** essentially none (goodwill $42.2M); no value destruction [SEC][1].
- **Dilution/SBC:** TTM SBC ~$338M (12.2% of revenue), **down from $801.6M in FY2024** (IPO-year grants, incl. $577.5M in Q1'24) and $343.2M in FY2025 [SEC][2]. Share count grew 8.4% y/y (buyback yield −8.4%) [SA][3]. Dilution is real but decelerating; management has not bought back stock despite $2.77B net cash — conservative, arguably too conservative at these prices.
- **Cash accumulation:** $2.79B cash+investments, ~$94M/yr interest income — fine, but a large net-cash drag on ROE.
- **Incentives:** SBC is the dominant comp vehicle (normal for growth-tech); targets are revenue/margin-driven. No dividends.

**Management Quality Rating: Good.** Capital allocation is disciplined; the open questions are pace of monetization (ARPU vs user experience) and the Google relationship, not capital waste.

## 5. Accounting & Earnings Normalization

This is where Reddit's reported numbers mislead. Three big adjustments:

1. **Stock-based compensation.** TTM SBC ≈ **$338M** (FY2025 $343.2M; H1'26 $169.3M vs H1'25 $174.5M; Q2'26 $101.0M) [SEC][2]. It is a real economic cost of the employee model. GAAP op income already includes it; **FCF does not** (OCF adds it back). Economic FCF = $1,019M − ~$338M ≈ **$681M** (24.5% margin).
2. **Taxes.** TTM effective rate 0.78% (tax expense $6.9M cash; Q2'26 1.55%) because of NOLs/valuation allowance [SA][3]. Normalized rate on future profits: **24%** (21% federal + ~3% state — *estimate*). Normalized NOPAT = $785M × 0.76 ≈ **$597M** vs reported net income $871M.
3. **R&D capitalization.** R&D is ~$810M annualized (~29% of revenue) and all expensed [SA][13]. For a company whose product is software + AI (search, recommendations, moderation), a 3-year straight-line capitalization would add roughly $500–600M of capitalized asset and raise normalized operating income ~$100–150M/yr (*estimate*). This is a genuine reinvestment — it partially offsets the SBC drag; I fold it into my margin assumptions rather than double-count.

**Normalized operating picture:** ~$2.78B revenue, ~28.5% GAAP op margin → **~26% economic after-tax margin** after normalized taxes and SBC-as-cash. ROIC on ex-cash operating capital ($0.5–0.7B): >100% reported-style; I model **90% declining to 30%**. Cross-holdings, pensions, leases, minority interests: immaterial. Contingent liability: ongoing AI-scraping litigation (defensive posture; also a revenue-licensing opportunity).

## 6. Growth Analysis

**Decomposition:** (a) **Market growth** — digital ads +AI data, high-single to low-double digits; (b) **share/price** — Reddit's ARPU is far below Meta's; most of its 66% growth is **price and ad-load expansion on a fixed audience** plus continued DAUq growth; (c) **new products** — conversational search ads, video, AI licensing; (d) **geography** — ROW only 20% of revenue.

**Quality of growth:** High quality — each incremental dollar of revenue requires ~0.3% of revenue in capex. But growth is **fragile at the traffic level**: DAUq missed Q2'26 estimates because of a Google algorithm change [12]. Revenue growth can continue while user growth stalls (ARPU), which is what makes the bear case revenue-mild but multiple-fatal.

**Fundamental growth:** Expected growth = reinvestment rate × ROIC. With ROIC ≈ 90% on incremental capital, modest reinvestment sustains high growth — the formula flatters asset-light networks. Realistically: **growth is capped by ad-market share gains and user growth**, not by capital. I model revenue growth decaying 5pts/yr from 35% (base) to 15% by year 5 — vs consensus +53.3% (2026) and +31.4% (2027) [SA][5] on a TTM-comparable basis.

**Growth duration:** High growth 2–3 years, transition years 3–5, stable 3% terminal. What slows growth: Google referral dependence, AI-search substitution, ad-load saturation, moderation/AI costs.

## 7. Risk Analysis

- **Business risk (HIGH):** ~50% of traffic via Google [12]; Google's AI Overviews and algorithm changes demonstrably move Reddit's user numbers (Q2'26 DAUq miss; −21% on 2026-07-30) [12][1]. Reported tension over renewal of the Google AI-content deal adds tail risk [12]. Competition: Meta/TikTok for ad budgets; every AI lab for corpus access. Regulatory: moderation liability, AI-scraping litigation both ways.
- **Financial risk (LOW):** $20.9M debt vs $2,786M cash; interest coverage n/a (positive interest income); fixed costs low. Failure/dilution risk: low-to-moderate (SBC ~12% of revenue, ~2%/yr share creep).
- **Valuation risk (HIGH):** At $151.71 the price embeds a bull path (§12). Value is highly sensitive to growth (see §14): ±10pts of Y1 growth moves IV by ~±$20.
- **Failure risk:** probability of business-model failure near zero; probability of **permanent impairment of the current price** (multi-year drawdown toward intrinsic value) is the real risk — I estimate 60–70% the stock trades below $110 at some point on Google-driven derating (*estimate*).

## 8. Cost of Capital & Required Return

- **Riskfree rate:** 4.74% (US 10Y T, used in Damodaran's Aug 1, 2026 ERP update) [9].
- **ERP:** 4.28% implied (TTM, adjusted payout) [9].
- **Beta:** 2.03 (5Y regression) [SA][3] → CoE = 4.74% + 2.03×4.28% = **13.4%**. However, a 5Y regression beta for a stock that IPO'd in 2024 and swung from hype (282.95 high) to derating (−37% 52-week) is mechanically inflated; peer betas are 0.91 (PINS), 1.02 (SNAP), 1.24 (META) [SA][6][7][8]. I use an **adjusted forward beta of 1.7** (judgment: midway between regression and peers, reflecting real Google-concentration risk) → CoE = 4.74% + 1.7×4.28% ≈ **12.0%**.
- **Cost of debt:** immaterial (~5% pre-tax on $21M). **Capital structure:** 99.9% equity; net cash 9.5% of market cap.
- **WACC ≈ 12.0%** (base; 11.0–13.5% scenario range). Compare ROIC ≈ 90%+ → growth is **strongly value-creating** today; the spread compresses toward 30% terminal ROIC.

## 12. Reverse DCF — What Does $151.71 Assume?

Holding my base assumptions (margins 28.5%→34%, tax 24%, reinvestment per ROIC, TV at 3%) and solving for first-year growth:

- **At WACC 12.0%:** price-implied Y1 revenue growth ≈ **53%** (Y1 revenue ≈ $4.25B — ~26% above the $3.38B consensus), then ≈48% → 43% → 38% → 33% over the horizon.
- **At WACC 13.4% (raw beta):** implied Y1 growth ≈ **59%**, sustained.
- Equivalently at consensus growth (~35% Y1), the price implies a WACC of **~9.9%** — a ~2pt haircut to an already modest 12% (i.e., the market is pricing Reddit at near-mature-company risk).

Verdict: **aggressive-to-nearly-impossible**. Reddit must compound at bull-case rates for five years *while its single largest traffic source (Google) is actively re-platforming search toward AI answers*. Are these assumptions reasonable? Only if you believe Google referrals stabilize and ARPU continues compounding at Meta-like rates. I assign that path (optimistic + bull) ~30% probability.

## 15. Market Expectations vs Fundamental Reality

- **A. Price:** $151.71 = 35.4× TTM EPS, 25.1× fwd, 10.5× sales, 28.7× FCF [SA][3].
- **B. Price-implied fundamentals:** ~53% Y1 revenue growth (26% above consensus), ~34% terminal margins, ~10% discount rate (§12).
- **C. My forecast:** 35% Y1 growth decaying to 15%, 28.5%→34% margins over the horizon, 12% WACC → **$109.50** base / $141.61 weighted.

**Price ≈ weighted value, but 28% above the base case.** The market is treating Reddit's bull-case execution (Google stabilization + Meta-like ARPU compounding) as the expected outcome, priced at a maturity-adjusted discount rate. Why might the market be wrong? (1) Recency bias — 66% growth extrapolated; (2) S&P 500 index inclusion (effective 2026-08-18) has temporarily flattered demand and the float [10][11]; (3) sell-side PTs ($215.63 avg) are anchored to fwd EPS ~$7.56 [SA][5] that ignores normalized taxes and SBC economics; (4) the Google relationship is a **known-unknown priced as a known-known**.

## 16. Real Options & Optionality

Real optionality here is genuine and mostly uncaptured by the base DCF: **AI data licensing at scale** (the $153.5M "other" line could compound if labs pay for fresh corpora — the bull scenario's +0.2 ROIC/+20pt growth edge), **conversational search ads**, **international (20% of revenue today)**, and **video**. These are not free options: each requires Google cooperation or displaces it. I price them through scenario probabilities (+~$12/share of weighted value from optimistic/bull tails) rather than bolting on a fantasy number.

## 18. Valuation Error & Uncertainty

**Confidence: Medium.** Estimation uncertainty dominates: beta (2.03 vs 1.7 → ±15% on value), terminal margin (34% ±5pts), and Google-dependent growth duration are each first-order. Economic uncertainty is unusually high for a company this profitable — a single external algorithm change moved the equity by 21% in a day. Model uncertainty is modest (the business is a clean ad-tech P&L). Treat $109.50 base and $141.61 weighted as centers of wide bands (~±30%), i.e., roughly **$75–195**.

## 19. Inversion: What Would Make This Investment Fail?

1. **Google turns competitor on distribution** — AI Overviews answer Reddit-style questions directly and search referrals structurally decline; DAUq stagnates, ARPU growth can't offset, terminal growth ≤0%. (Bear scenario, $45.27.)
2. **AI disruption of the corpus moat** — labs find Reddit's data increasingly substitutable (synthetic data, other communities); the licensing revenue line never compounds while ad margins compress from AI-moderation costs.
3. **Margin plateau** — SBC re-accelerates (AI talent wars), moderation costs rise, and the 28.5%→34% ramp stalls at ~28% while growth decays → conservative scenario ($70.40).
4. **Multiple derating without business failure** — the price embeds ~26% more Y1 revenue growth than consensus; even *meeting* consensus for two years could mean a flat-to-down stock (value converges from $151.71 toward ~$109).

**Three assumptions whose failure would do the most damage:** (1) Google traffic stability; (2) durable 30%+ revenue compounding; (3) operating margin reaching ~32%+ without an SBC blow-up.
