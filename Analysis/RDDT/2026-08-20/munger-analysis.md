# Charlie Munger Lens — Reddit, Inc. (NYSE: RDDT)

**Reference price $151.71 (2026-08-19 close). Market cap $29.19B. 192.4M shares.**[7]

## Bottom Line

**Rating: C — WATCH at $151.71.** Reddit is close to a wonderful business: an asset-light text-community network compounding 66.6% TTM revenue growth, 91.4% gross margins, 36.7% FCF margins, DAUq of 130.3M growing 18%, and a data corpus ("26+ billion posts and comments") that both search engines and AI labs now pay to license.[8][3][2] It is also, on my arithmetic, a stock priced for a future that arithmetic cannot produce. Munger's discipline — discount at WACC+3%, terminal growth ≤2%, and treat stock-based compensation as a real cost — puts intrinsic value at **bear $27 / base $40 / bull $50, weighted ≈ $35**, because true owner economics after SBC are only ~$3.54/share, not the reported FCF of $5.30/share. At $151.71 the market is paying **~42x owner earnings and ~29x FCF** and implicitly betting that net-of-SBC cash earnings compound at **~14% per year forever** (or ~31%/yr for a decade) at a 16.5% hurdle — a bet I will not make at this price even for a business I admire.[8][2][4] "It's far better to buy a wonderful company at a fair price than a fair company at a wonderful price." This is the wonderful company at a wonderful price, and wonderful prices are how wonderful companies become mediocre investments. Watchlist; buy below ~$45, conviction below ~$35.

## 1. Circle of Competence: Edge

The business is easy to state: people form communities around interests; the platform sells their attention to advertisers and licenses their accumulated conversation to AI labs; volunteers moderate it; hosting costs almost nothing relative to revenue.[3][2] That part I understand cold — and so do the unit economics: 91.3% gross margin, capex of $6.7M against $2.2B of FY25 revenue, a balance sheet that is mostly marketable securities.[3][4] What sits at the boundary of my competence is the interaction between Reddit and generative AI search: Google is simultaneously Reddit's largest free-traffic source, its largest advertising rival, the entity whose AI Overviews can answer queries without a click, *and* a paying data-licensing customer (long-term content-license performance obligations of $92.1M beyond one year).[2][4] No one — not management, not the analysts, not Google — knows how that four-way lollapalooza resolves, which is precisely why a Munger-style discount rate and a Munger-style margin of safety are non-negotiable here. **Rating: Edge.** I can value the cash flows; I cannot predict the referral function.

## 2. Mental Models Applied

**1. Network effects and critical mass (physics/economics) — the moat that is real.** 130.3M DAUq (+18% y/y), 26+ billion posts and comments, and a community structure where each new niche subreddit deepens the corpus.[2][3] The archive is the competitive asset that cannot be re-created: every AI licensing contract sold (the "other revenue" line, $153.5M TTM) monetizes twenty years of accumulated, hard-to-replicate human conversation.[8][2] This is a genuine network effect with critical mass reached long ago.

**2. Incentives (psychology) — mostly aligned, with one expensive habit.** Steve Huffman votes ~75% of the voting power through agreements with Advance (~22% owner) and Tencent affiliates, yet took $3.1M of total 2025 compensation and runs an 11.3:1 CEO pay ratio — founder-owner economics without founder-owner extraction.[4][5] The expensive habit is the company-wide comp model: SBC of $338M TTM (12.2% of revenue), down from $801.6M in FY2024 but still the difference between reported FCF and true owner earnings.[2][4] The workforce is paid in shares, the company's favorite metric (Adjusted EBITDA, $342.8M in Q2'26 alone) adds the cost back, and shareholders are diluted ~1.5% per half-year gross, only partially offset by buybacks.[2][3]

**3. Inversion on the price (a Munger special) — what must be true for $151.71 to be fair.** Working backwards: with net-of-SBC owner economics of $3.54/share, a 16.5% discount rate (WACC ~13.5% + 3%), and 2% terminal growth, the price embeds ~14%/year growth in true cash earnings **forever** — or ~31%/year for ten years.[6][7][8] Even at the market's own 13.5% cost of equity it implies ~11% perpetual growth. At 28.7x trailing FCF, the market has already awarded Reddit the decade it still has to earn.[7]

**4. Opportunity cost and regression to the mean (economics/mathematics).** TTM revenue growth of 66.6% is a young-monetization artifact (ARPU $6.18, +36%) and will decelerate — DAU growth is already only 18%.[8][3] When growth halves toward a durable rate, every ratio regresses with it: a 20-30% grower does not keep a 28.7x FCF multiple, and a 2.03-beta stock does not deserve a sub-15% cost of capital.[7] Meanwhile the risk-free alternative pays 4.65% (10-yr, 2026-08-19) with zero lollapalooza risk.[6]

**5. Symbiosis and friction (biology/physics).** Reddit's symbiosis with its moderators — hundreds of thousands of unpaid volunteers — is the lowest-cost content-governance system on the internet and the reason margins look like a software company's. Friction is near zero: 91.4% gross margin, $6.7M capex.[8][4] But symbiosis with volunteers is a fragile asset — it erodes if the platform over-commercializes or over-automates, and it is not on the balance sheet.

## 3. Inversion: How This Investment Fails

**Failure mode 1 — Google turns from tailwind to headwind.** The stock fell ~21% on Q2'26 earnings (2026-07-30) on precisely this concern, and a securities class action plus shareholder derivative suits alleging misstatements about Google Search and AI Overviews have been filed.[7][2] Organic-search referral is a material, unpaid, algorithmically-governed traffic source; Reddit's own 10-Q admits its machine-translated content has already been deprioritized by search engines.[2] If Google's ranking of Reddit content fades structurally, DAU growth goes from 18% to single digits — and my bear case becomes the base.

**Failure mode 2 — AI answers replace Reddit visits faster than licensing replaces ads.** The licensing revenue stream is real but small: "other" revenue is $153.5M TTM, ~5.5% of the total, against $2,625M of advertising.[8] If AI Overviews absorb the search queries that used to land on Reddit, the lost ad inventory is an order of magnitude larger than the licensing fees gained — unless licensing scales ~10x, which is a hope, not a number.

**Failure mode 3 — the SBC treadmill.** Gross share issuance ran ~2.95M shares in H1'26 (~1.5% per half-year) against a 1.52M-share buyback; the company retired stock at ~$157 while my base value is $40.[2] Every year of RSU vesting at hypergrowth valuations is value transferred from shareholders to employees at peak prices — Munger's "whose cost is it?" question, asked with real money.

**Failure mode 4 — paying 10.5x sales for a decelerating 2.03-beta asset.** The stock is already −46% from its 52-week high of $282.95; the S&P 500 inclusion spike (+16.5% on 08-14) faded within days to $151.71.[9][10][7] Inclusion and Buy ratings (33 analysts, avg PT $215.63) are not earnings — they are social proof. If growth decelerates one notch below expectations, 28.7x FCF can become 15x on no change in the business, and $151.71 becomes ~$85 without a single operating disappointment.[7]

**Failure mode 5 — regulatory and community integrity.** A £14.5M UK ICO GDPR penalty (February 2026, under appeal), minors-safety regimes worldwide, and the permanent fragility of a volunteer-moderated platform facing AI-generated content.[2] None of these kills the company alone; each raises the probability of growth-hampering restrictions.

**Bias self-check (from the 25):** *Anchoring* — "down 46% from the high" whispers cheap; I have refused the anchor and valued from owner earnings. *Social proof* — S&P inclusion, 33 Buy ratings, a $215.63 average target: all herding artifacts, none arithmetic. *Confirmation bias* — I want to own this one (network effect, 91% gross margin, net cash); the −77% expected-value math is therefore a discipline, not an opinion. *Loss aversion* — the reflex after a −21% gap-down is to "average in"; the discipline is to wait for $45. *Overconfidence* — the AI era could make the corpus *more* valuable; I grant that in the bull case, and it still fails at this price.

**Avoid-stupidity checklist:** Don't chase at high valuations — 28.7x FCF / 10.5x sales on hypergrowth ✓ flagged. Don't buy dependence on a single major counterparty — Google is traffic, rival, AI-summary threat, and customer at once ✓ flagged. Don't invest in businesses I cannot predict — Edge, and the AI-search interaction is explicitly unpredictable ✓ flagged. Don't buy businesses whose moat is eroding — the moat is *deepening* today, the only test Reddit passes cleanly ✓. Don't invest outside competence ✓. Don't buy mediocre-just-because-cheap ✓ (it is excellent; it is not cheap). **The only stupidity left on the table is paying $151.71 for a $40 base case.**

## 5. Earnings-Quality Tests

- **OCF vs NI: PASS (borderline).** TTM OCF $1,026M vs NI $871.1M = **1.18x** against the >1.2x standard; FY2025 was 1.30x ($690.9M / $529.7M). Quality is fine; the test that matters is the next one.[8][4]
- **SBC — the real earnings test: FAIL if untreated.** SBC: $47.6M (2023) → $801.6M (2024, IPO year) → $343.2M (2025) → $338.0M TTM (H1'26 $169.3M vs H1'25 $174.5M) = **12.2% of TTM revenue and 38.8% of TTM net income**.[2][4] FCF of $1,019M TTM *adds SBC back*; true owner economics ≈ $681M ($3.54/share). The company's headline non-GAAP metric (Adjusted EBITDA, $342.8M in Q2'26, +106% y/y) excludes $106.8M of Q2 SBC-and-taxes — the trend is improving, but the excluded item is the cost of the workforce, and Munger counts it.[3][2]
- **Accounts receivable: healthy.** $650.1M at 6/30/26 vs $590.2M at 12/31/25 (+10% H1 against far faster revenue growth); deferred revenue $38.6M; performance obligations >1yr only $92.1M.[2]
- **CapEx & depreciation: immaterial.** Capex $6.7M vs D&A ~$15.9M (FY25) — no maintenance-capex games possible.[4]
- **Goodwill: clean.** $42.2M = 1.3% of equity, far below the 30% alarm.[2]
- **Balance sheet: fortress.** Total debt $20.9M against cash+investments $2,786M → net cash $2,765M ($13.63/share); interest coverage is moot (the company earns net interest income, $47.9M H1'26).[8][2]
- **Share count: the leak.** Class A 145.84M + Class B 46.48M = 192.32M shares at 6/30/26 vs 190.89M at 12/31/25 — a net +1.43M *after* 1,523,502 shares were repurchased for $239.6M (~$157.27 avg); gross issuance ≈ 2.95M shares in six months.[2]
- **Tax: one quirk to know.** A full U.S. valuation allowance still sits on deferred tax assets; a release would print a large one-time accounting benefit — quality scorekeepers should exclude it when it comes.[2]
- **Owner earnings (TTM, est.):** NI $871.1M + D&A ~$16M − capex ~$7M − working-capital build ~$190M (est.) ≈ **$690M ≈ $3.59/share** — essentially the same as FCF minus SBC, and the honest base for the DCF below. At $151.71 the market pays **~42x owner earnings**.[8][2][4]

## 6. Valuation: Conservative DCF

Per Munger doctrine: discount = WACC + 3%, terminal growth ≤ 2%. WACC estimate: rf 4.65% (10-yr, 08/19/2026) + ERP ~4.4% (est.) × beta 2.03 ≈ 13.6% cost of equity; debt is negligible → **WACC ≈ 13.5% (est.) → central discount 16.5%**.[6][7] Ten-year model on $3.54/share of net-of-SBC owner economics (FCF $1,019M − SBC $338M, ÷ 192.4M shares).[8][2][4]

| Scenario | Path (owner economics/share, 10 yrs) | r | tg | **IV/share** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Conservative (severe bear) | $3.54 +2%/yr (AI answers blunt discovery; DAU stalls) | 17.5% | 1.5% | **$23** |
| Neutral (base) | $3.54 +10%/yr (growth decelerates from ~67% toward high-teens; SBC stays ~12%) | 16.5% | 2.0% | **$40** |
| Optimistic (bull) | $3.54 +15%/yr yrs 1–5, +8%/yr yrs 6–10 (licensing scales; ads compound) | 15.5% | 2.0% | **$50** |

**Cross-checks.** (a) *Generous variant:* even using raw FCF of $5.30/share and ignoring SBC entirely, base = $60 and bull = $76 — still 50–60% below the quote. (b) *Reverse DCF:* $151.71 implies net-of-SBC owner earnings compounding ~14%/yr **forever** at 16.5% (or ~31%/yr for ten years then 2%), and ~11%/yr forever even at the market's own 13.5% rate. (c) *Relative:* 35.4x trailing EPS, 25.1x forward, 10.5x sales, 28.7x FCF — every multiple is a hypergrowth multiple, while DAU growth is already 18%.[7][8]

**Margin of safety: FAILS.** Price $151.71 vs base $40 = +279% (the market cap exceeds my base IV by ~$21.5B). Against weighted ≈ $35 the expected value is **≈ −77%**. Munger's rule — intrinsic value 30–40% above price — is inverted by a factor of four. No dividend exists to soften it. The one honorable excuse for holding would be "the moat is deepening and I will never find my entry" — but with a 2.03 beta, entry windows are a question of *when*, not *if*.[7]

## 8. Risks

1. **Google four-way dependency.** Traffic source + advertising rival + AI-summary disintermediator + licensing customer. The Q2'26 selloff and the pending securities class action exist because this dependency is both real and unquantifiable.[7][2]
2. **AI cannibalization.** Licensing revenue ($153.5M TTM) cannot yet replace ad impressions if AI Overviews absorb search demand — a 10x scale-up would be required for parity.[8]
3. **SBC dilution treadmill.** ~3%/yr gross issuance; the $1.0B buyback (43% used) merely offsets half at rich prices.[2]
4. **Valuation compression.** 28.7x FCF on 66.6% growth; if growth halves, the multiple halves first — the stock is already −46% from its high on *one* bad earnings reaction.[8][7]
5. **Regulatory and platform integrity.** UK ICO £14.5M GDPR penalty under appeal; global minors/content regulation; volunteer-moderator model's fragility to AI-generated content and over-monetization.[2]

## 9. Final Rating: C — WATCH

The company passes the quality tests Munger cares most about — network-effect moat that is still deepening, 91% gross margins, ~zero capital requirements, net cash of $2.77B, founder alignment with modest extraction — and it fails exactly one, the one that compounds: **the price.** $151.71 pays ~42x owner earnings for a 2.03-beta asset whose single biggest input (Google referral) is outside its control and currently in question. My arithmetic says fair value is roughly a quarter of the quote, and no scenario I can build justifies the quote today. I do not short it — the moat is real and shorting network effects is expensive — but I buy only at a Munger price. **Buy zone: <$45 (starter), <$35 (conviction); hold $45–100 for existing owners only if the Q3'26 print clears Google concerns; reduce ≥$150.** Patience is the edge; the 2.03 beta guarantees the market will offer the entry price again.[7]
