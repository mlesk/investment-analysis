I would get **aggressive on confirmation**: Neutron safely on the pad / successful first launch, or a 10-year rollover below ~4.3%, or a dislocation toward **$50–65**. **Rating: C — Monitor.**

# 1. Start With the Macro, Not the Company

## Growth

- **Real activity:** The FOMC describes "economic activity… expanding at a solid pace" with strong productivity and capital investment, while citing "elevated uncertainty" from the Middle East conflict. No recession in the base case over the next 12 months; a modest growth deceleration is the most likely path as high real rates and an energy shock bite. ([FED][5])
- **Implication for RKLB:** Rocket Lab's revenue is ~2/3 Space Systems (satellite manufacturing, government programs) and ~1/3 launch. Government/defense demand (NSSL Lane 1 ceiling tripled to $17B, Golden Dome-era HASTE, SBAMT/Flatellite, SDA Tranche 2/3) is **counter-cyclical and budget-grounded** — a genuine insulation from a growth slowdown. Commercial launch demand (constellation operators, "launch has never been so constrained") is structural and booked years out. RKLB is one of the few equities whose demand is _improving_ while the cycle softens. ([TR][2])

## Inflation

- **Headline:** July CPI +3.4% y/y (down from 3.5%); +0.1% m/m after a -0.4% June. ([BLS][4])
- **Core:** all-items-less-food-and-energy +2.5% y/y (down from 2.6%) — still disinflating, within striking distance of target. ([BLS][4])
- **The problem child is energy:** energy +14.7% y/y (gasoline +24.6% y/y) — a supply shock tied to the Middle East conflict; the FOMC explicitly cites "supply shocks that have driven price increases in certain sectors, including energy" and says "inflation remains elevated." ([BLS][4], [FED][5])
- **Implication for RKLB:** Core disinflation is a slow tailwind; headline energy inflation is a fast headwind via the discount rate. For a stock priced on 2028–2030 cash flows, the **discount-rate channel dominates** — every 25–50bp of upward rate pressure mechanically compresses the multiple. This is the single most important macro fact for the stock's -47% drawdown.

## Monetary Policy

- **Fed funds target:** **3.50–3.75%**, maintained July 29, 2026 by a **9–3 vote** — Hammack, Kashkari, and Logan dissented in favor of a **25bp hike**. The Committee says it "will deliver price stability." ([FED][5])
- **The regime change:** Kevin Warsh is now Fed Chair. The market is explicitly bracing for a possible hike: Kashkari says "now is the time to start slowly moving rates up"; Posen says hikes are "needed but won't come before December"; the Fed is even contemplating fewer meetings. This is a materially more hawkish regime than the 2024–25 easing cycle. ([CNBC][7])
- **Fed path (my estimate):** base case is an extended hold, with a non-trivial tail that the Fed **hikes 25bp** if oil/energy keeps pushing headline inflation higher; the first _cut_ is unlikely before late 2026 – mid 2027, and only once the energy shock fades and core inflation is durably ~2.3–2.5%. For a long-duration space stock, the risk skew is to higher rates near-term.

## Liquidity (detailed in Section 2)

- QT is over; the Fed balance sheet is ~$6.76T and +$116B over 52 weeks; the ON RRP is drained (~$0.7B); reserve balances are ample (~$2.9T); the TGA has rebuilt to ~$1.0T (a mild, likely temporary, fiscal drain). Net: **stable to modestly expanding** — a mild tailwind for risk assets, but it is _not_ flooding and does not offset the discount-rate headwind. ([FED][9])

## Fiscal Policy

- U.S. deficit financing continues to weigh on the long end: 10Y yields have drifted from ~4.0% in January to ~4.69% in August despite a _stable_ policy rate — i.e., the move is term premium / supply / energy-driven, not Fed-driven. ([UST][6], [CNBC][7])
- **The tailwind for RKLB:** defense and industrial-policy spending is a direct fiscal beneficiary — the Pentagon is accelerating space procurement (NSSL ceiling $5.6B → $17B; SBAMT/Flatellite $397M; record $266M HASTE), and Europe is rearming (Germany alone put >$40B toward a satellite missile-warning system; Rocket Lab Germany is being stood up). Space is a **defense-fiscal winner** in a world of rising defense budgets. ([TR][2])

## Global Macro

- Strong dollar (yen-intervention chatter), a Middle East energy shock, and record gold (~$4,454) tell the same story: **geopolitical risk premium + supply shock**. For RKLB this cuts both ways — higher defense budgets (positive) but higher discount rates (negative). The net macro push is negative for the multiple near-term. ([UST][6])

### The dominant macro force driving asset prices right now:

> **The bond market / discount rate.** The 10-year at ~4.69% (52-week high 4.88% set Aug 13, 2026) is signaling term-premium pressure, energy-driven inflation, and heavy supply — CNBC's bond desk is literally publishing "The bond market is signaling trouble ahead for stocks." ([CNBC][7])

### Will that force strengthen, weaken, or reverse over 6–18 months?

**My estimate: it stays elevated and can overshoot higher before easing.** Higher-for-longer on the front end plus heavy supply and an energy shock argue the 10-year tests **4.8–5.0%** before core disinflation wins and it rolls back toward **4.3–4.5%** late in the window. For RKLB this means the multiple headwind is likely to _persist through the Neutron launch window_ — the worst possible timing coincidence.

# 6. Earnings Expectations & Revision Cycle

- **Consensus:** 18 analysts, **Buy**, avg PT **$112.94** (range $64–150). FY26 revenue est $951M; FY27 $1.35B; FY26 adj. EPS −$0.05 → FY27 adj. EPS +$0.05 (first profitable year on an adjusted basis). ([FP][15])

This is the classic Druckenmiller "**earnings improving while the stock falls because expectations for the _discount rate_ are even higher**" configuration — the market is de-rating the _multiple_, not the _fundamentals_.
- **Q2 was a mixed-but-underappreciated print:** revenue $234M (+62% y/y, at guide), GAAP GM 36.1% (above 33–35% guide), NG GM 41.5% (above 38–40%), adj. EBITDA −$8.8M (well above −$20 to −$26M guide) — but **GAAP EPS −$0.08 missed the −$0.06 consensus** on Mynaric amortization, and Q3 guidance implies a **gross-margin step-down (29–31% GAAP)** from mix. - **Why the Q3 GM guide is actually a bullish tell:** management frames it as mix-driven (satellite platforms + Mynaric integration) with a "beneficial remixing impact… as we look beyond Q3" — i.e., they are **kitchen-sinking Q3 ahead of the Neutron launch** so the post-launch re-acceleration shows up clean. The pattern (guide down, deliver, raise) is the mark of disciplined management (SolAero precedent: margins from high-single-digits to in-line within ~a year).
- **Estimate dispersion:** low — the Street is nearly unanimous; only "one analyst urges caution" per TipRanks. ([TP][10]) This unanimity is itself a risk: if Neutron slips, there is no low bar to beat.
- **Revision-cycle phase:** **Mid-cycle, positive-but-macro-capped.** Fundamentals accelerating (revenue, backlog, bookings); the cap is the discount rate, not the company.

# 10. Variant Perception & The "What's Priced In?" Test
