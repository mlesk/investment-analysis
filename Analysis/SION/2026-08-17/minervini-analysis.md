**Rating: F — Fundamentally and technically unsuitable (Do not buy)**

**Verdict:** Under Minervini's Specific Entry Point Analysis, SION fails every critical criterion at once. It has no earnings, no revenue, and no fundamental acceleration to underwrite. Technically it is in a confirmed Stage 4 downtrend — trading ~85% below its 50/100/200-day moving averages, near its 52-week low, with a completely broken base and a 14-day RSI of 21.5 (deeply oversold). ## 1. Minervini Opportunity Classification & Eligibility

**Is this a growth company with accelerating earnings/sales?** No. Sionna is pre-revenue with no earnings; H1-26 net loss widened to −$56.7M and R&D spend rose y/y, but there is no top-line to accelerate. [SIONNA][1]

**Is it a market or industry leader?** No. It is a challenger to Vertex (Trikafta standard of care; CF franchise ~$10B+/yr, estimate) whose flagship program just failed [SIONNA][2][YAHOO][4]. It is one of the worst-performing biotechs of the last year (YTD −85.6%).

**Is it in a confirmed Stage 2 advance?** No — this is a textbook **Stage 4 decline**: it made a major high (~$55 within the past year), broke down on heavy volume after the 8/10/26 failure, and is now ~89% below that high and ~85% below all its major moving averages [YAHOO][4][TECH][6].

**Is institutional demand apparent?** No. The stock collapsed on elevated volume (2.9M shares vs 1.6M average) — distribution, not accumulation. Large 13G/13D holders exist from the IPO era but that is ownership, not current sponsorship.

**Is there a constructive pattern / catalyst / entry point?** No. The only catalyst is negative; there is no base, no pivot, and no low-risk entry.

**Minervini Eligibility Rating: F — Fundamentally or technically unsuitable.**
