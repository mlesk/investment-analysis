03The Debate: Agreement, Contention, Resolution

Round 1: opening statements. Round 2: uncontested common ground. Round 3: six disputed points, cross-examined to resolution. Round 4: final concessions.

Uncontested consensus core

This is a genuinely dominant franchise. Largest US mortgage lender AND largest servicer ($2T UPB), ~70% recurring or less rate-sensitive revenue, Redfin search funnel (50M MAU, 47% attach), home equity, Rocket Loans/Money — a real moat (scale × servicing recapture × distribution × AI × IG balance sheet), moderate not wide.
The servicing cash floor is real. ~$1B/quarter of steady servicing cash flow on a $2T book; 57% of refi closes come from existing serviced clients at near-zero acquisition cost.
The balance sheet is a fortress by industry standards. 0.9x net corporate leverage, $11.2B liquidity (+$1.8B QoQ), the only IG-rated public US mortgage company — the GAAP −$29.7B 'net cash' is a matched-asset artifact.
SBC is the per-share tax. ~$430M TTM of stock-based compensation ≈ 91% of GAAP net income ($471M) — the single most consequential earnings-quality judgment; GAAP net margin 4.6% vs adjusted ~16% is the crux.
The refi option is real but dated. $320B (>6% note rate, 26% of owned MSR) + 14.3% refi share + $300B capacity = a fat, credible right tail — a 2021-like refi wave, likely an H1-2027 catalyst, priced at $0 today.
No margin of safety at $14.50. All five independently found the price at or above their base value (Buffett base $9, Damodaran $8.24, Munger $5.50; Druckenmiller/Soros base ~flat-to-positive only on the option).
2.83B shares is the 2025 recap count, not dilution. The four-class → two-class up-C collapse reflects full economic ownership; all five use 2.83B for per-share valuation.
One valuation-critical variable. Economic EBIT margin (17→20% after SBC + intangibles amortization) and incremental ROIC vs the ~9% WACC — if the GAAP/adjusted gap never closes, share gains destroy value.
Unanimous refusal to buy at $14.50. Three C's, one C+ Monitor, one B (asymmetric, speculative, scale-in) — no profile buys at full size at $14.50; no short is recommended either.

Contested points — and how they were resolved

Reference price and intrinsic value vs 12-month price. Buffett/Munger/Damodaran frame scenario values as intrinsic value today; Druckenmiller/Soros frame theirs as 12–18-month price expectations (Druckenmiller weighted ~$15–15.50, Soros ~$16.73). Cross-examination: these are different objects — consensus base IV (~$8) sits far below the price while the 12-month price expectations cluster at/above $14.50; Druckenmiller and Soros both concede their cells are price paths, not claims about fundamental worth. Named concession: Druckenmiller concedes 'my base is a price forecast under a stationary macro, not an intrinsic value'; Soros concedes his 'B' is a reflexivity trade on expectations, not a value anchor. Resolved: the three IV models set the consensus IV; Druckenmiller/Soros inform the near-term ladder and triggers only.
Where does base-case intrinsic value actually sit? Buffett ~$9 (normalized owner earnings $1.1–2.4B), Damodaran $8.24 (verified workbook: 17→20% economic EBIT margin, 9% WACC, 3% terminal growth, ~$2.3B net debt), Munger $5.50 (13.3% discount, 2% terminal growth, SBC-adjusted). Cross-examination: Munger challenges Buffett and Damodaran for capitalizing adjusted, pre-SBC earnings; Buffett concedes his ~$9 assumes demonstrated normalized owner earnings; Damodaran challenges Munger's WACC+3% as double-punishing risk. Named concession: Munger concedes 'at a normal 9–10% discount my base rises toward $6–8.50'; Damodaran concedes Munger's SBC point is already the hinge of his own margin. Resolved: consensus base ≈ ~$8/share — the intersection of Buffett and Damodaran, with Munger as the conservative floor.
How much credit for the right tail (the refi wave)? Damodaran extreme bull $73.62 @ 10%; Munger extreme $12.50 @ 5% (refuses to pay for the option); Buffett bull $17, refuses to weight it; Druckenmiller extreme bull $21–24 (a 2027 call); Soros extreme bull $26 @ 8% (largest levered call on rate relief priced at $0). Cross-examination: all agree the tail is real ($320B >6% MSR, 14.3% refi share, 57% recapture, $300B capacity) but fat and not likely — the probability-weighted consensus (~$14.86) is lifted entirely by the extreme tail (~$7.36 of $14.86). Named concession: Munger concedes 'this is the most credible optionality in the industry — I simply refuse to pay for it in advance.' Resolved: consensus bull ~$18, extreme bull ~$73.62 (adopted workbook tail as the option proxy), combined ~25% probability — the tail justifies watching closely, not paying for it.
What price compensates for the uncertainty? Buffett first interest ≤$10 / accumulate $6–9 / conviction <$6; Munger starter <$8 / accumulate $5–6 / conviction <$4; Damodaran buy <$10 / add <$8 / sell >$18; Druckenmiller starter $11.50–13.50 (≤1–2%, stop <$11.50); Soros scale-in <$14.50, washout $12.5–13, sell >$20. Management/governance quality. Buffett 7.5/10 (integrity 8, allocation 6, ops 8); Munger 'execution good, incentives poor' (controller overhang, SBC-heavy comp); Damodaran 'Good — show me the returns' (stock-funded $14B+ M&A, SBC, 62% controller); Druckenmiller 'alignment high'; Soros 'defense is offense'. Cross-examination: the 2025 recap gave the controller ~62% voting power via Class L while Class A got a $0.80 special dividend; $430M SBC ≈ 91% of net income; $10.6B goodwill + $20.9B intangibles (TBV −$8.0B); but execution is ahead of plan (record share/margin, $100M synergies realized, $11.2B liquidity). Named concession: Munger concedes execution is genuinely excellent; Damodaran concedes balance-sheet discipline and candor are real; Buffett concedes capital strength partially offsets the governance concentration. Resolved: consensus 6.5/10 — high-integrity, operationally excellent, disciplined balance sheet; docked for founder control (~62%), SBC ≈ net income, and an unproven M&A thesis.
The net-debt / GAAP convention judgment. Damodaran uses ~$2.3B economic net corporate debt (0.9x EBITDA; excludes matched warehouse/MSR/Ginnie financing) — his 'single most important judgment', noting a pure-GAAP corporate view (~$7.5B) would cut value ~$1.8–2.0/share (~25%); Buffett ~$2.7B; Munger ~$2.3B; Druckenmiller/Soros cite 0.9x / $11.2B as the operating frame. Cross-examination: GAAP total debt $33.2B and 'net cash' −$29.7B are meaningless for a matched-balance-sheet mortgage platform; all five agree on the economic convention, but the exact number swings value ~25%. Named concession: Soros concedes the economic frame while flagging that a credit event could make the matched debt less matched. Resolved: consensus adopts the economic net-debt convention (~$2.3B corporate net debt, 0.9x EBITDA), disclosed; a pure-GAAP view would lower consensus base IV from ~$8 to ~$6–6.50 without changing the C rating.

06Pros & Cons — At $14.50

FOR the investment
  • Dominant platform — largest US mortgage lender AND servicer ($2T UPB), ~70% recurring or less rate-sensitive revenue; real, widening moat (scale × servicing recapture × Redfin × AI × IG balance sheet)
  • Record share gains in a shrinking market — purchase 6.2%, refi 14.3%, D2C purchase volume +45% YoY; 28% adjusted-EBITDA margin, most profitable quarter in four years
  • Fortress balance sheet — 0.9x net corporate leverage, $11.2B liquidity, only IG-rated public US mortgage company; ~$1B/quarter steady servicing cash flow
  • Fat right tail — $320B (>6% note rate) owned MSR, 57% recapture of serviced clients, $300B capacity: the largest levered call on a refi wave in the industry, priced at $0 (workbook extreme bull $73.62)
  • Integration ahead of plan — $100M Mr. Cooper synergies realized, $400M+ target plus ~$100M extra H1'27; Redfin attach 47% → 50% target, mortgage leads +2x YoY
  • Analyst consensus 'Buy' with PT $17.70 (+22%) — 17 analysts constructive; short interest declining; value funds accumulating
AGAINST the investment
  • No margin of safety — price ~81% above consensus base IV (~$8) and ≈ the probability-weighted value (~$14.86); the market already pays for a largely successful integration and a rate recovery
  • Earnings-quality chasm — SBC $430M TTM ≈ 91% of GAAP net income ($471M); GAAP net margin 4.6% vs adjusted ~16%; negative TTM FCF (−$772M); adjusted figures systematically flatter
  • Negative tangible book −$8.0B — $10.6B goodwill + $20.9B intangibles; an impairment to intangibles would hit book directly and is the bear's live weapon
  • Rate-cyclical core — 30-yr ~6.8% near a 1-yr high; Q3 market expected smaller than Q2 (first since 2022); the refi catalyst is dated (H1-2027), not imminent
  • Governance concentration — Dan Gilbert ~62% voting control via Class L; the 2025 recap centralised control; minority shareholders have no recourse
  • Rich multiple — ~20x forward on cyclical normalized earnings (vs PFSI ~8.8x, UWMC ~6.1x); 27.4x EV/EBITDA; beta 2.21 cuts both ways

07What Changes the Rating

Upgrade C → B/A when:
  1. Economic EBIT margin demonstrates 17→20% while the GAAP/adjusted gap closes — GAAP net margin toward ~10%+ and SBC as % of net income falls far below 91%
  2. Incremental ROIC on growth capital demonstrably exceeds the ~9% WACC — organic share-gain growth, not stock-funded M&A
  3. Normalized free cash flow turns sustainably positive — servicing cash (~$1B/qtr) converts into positive GAAP OCF/FCF over consecutive quarters
  4. A refi-cycle inflection arrives — 30-yr mortgage sustains below ~6.25% (the $320B >6% MSR pool starts converting; 14.3% refi share harvests it) with share gains holding
  5. A margin of safety returns — price into the $8–10 zone and/or share count stabilises while per-share earnings grow
Downgrade C → D/E if:
  1. A sustained rate shock — 30-yr mortgage above ~7.4% for 4+ weeks, revenue keeps falling and share gains stall (Soros/Druckenmiller falsification)
  2. A GAAP-quality reckoning — SBC permanently outruns net income (share count grows 4–5%/yr), GAAP margins stay ~5%, adjusted earnings never convert to cash
  3. An impairment or MSR hedge loss — a charge against the $31.5B goodwill+intangibles (TBV −$8.0B) or a UWMC-style hedge blow-up breaks the earnings-power frame
  4. Share-gain stall — purchase share flat-to-down for two quarters despite the Redfin funnel; GoS margins compress below ~300bps under pricing incentives
  5. The net-debt convention proves optimistic — warehouse/MSR financing becomes less matched in a credit event, exposing the GAAP $33.2B

09The Five Consensus Conclusions

The Business. Rocket Companies is the dominant US homeownership platform — the largest mortgage lender AND servicer ($2T UPB), with ~70% recurring or less rate-sensitive revenue, a Redfin search funnel, home equity, Rocket Loans and Rocket Money. The moat (scale × servicing recapture × distribution × AI × IG balance sheet) is real, widening and moderate — not wide. The business quality is not in dispute.
The Economics. The sole determinant of long-term per-share value is the economic EBIT margin (17→20%) and incremental ROIC vs the ~9% cost of capital — gated by ~$430M/yr of stock-based compensation (≈ 91% of net income) and the ~$440M/yr of acquired-intangible amortization treated as real costs. If the GAAP (4.6%) vs adjusted (~16%) gap never closes, record share gains destroy value.
The Market's Expectations. At $14.50 the market is implicitly assuming RKT roughly doubles revenue to ~$20B at ~20% EBIT margins — a return of the refinance cycle plus sustained share gains (the Bull plus part of the Extreme-Bull scenario). Aggressive but not impossible; the consensus 'Buy / $17.70' reflects it.
The Valuation. Consensus base intrinsic value ~$8/share; probability-weighted ~$14.86/share (≈ price, +2.5%); bear ~$2–4.50; bull ~$18 with an extreme tail $73.62. Damodaran workbook: $2.04 / $4.39 / $8.24 / $19.46 / $73.62, weighted $15.07. Confidence: Low/Medium. The base value is ~45% below the price; the weighted value barely clears it only because of the right tail.
The Investment Decision. C — Hold/Watch at $14.50; do not initiate. Begin accumulating at $8–10, build at $6–8, high conviction <$6 — provided the monitoring hierarchy (economic EBIT margin / incremental ROIC > SBC% > share gains > servicing cash flow > 30-yr rate) stays intact. Upgrades require proof; the operating data and the rate cycle, not the stock price, will deliver the verdict.

10Final Consensus Judgment

“

At a price of $14.50, the market is paying ~20x forward for a rate-cyclical financial whose normalized (SBC- and amortization-aware) earnings power supports a consensus base intrinsic value near ~$8 — roughly 45% below the price — and whose probability-weighted value (~$14.86) barely clears the price only because of the fat right tail: a 2021-like refi wave on a $2T servicing book, $320B of in-the-money MSRs, and 14.3% refi share. Five independent frameworks — owner-earnings value, multidisciplinary quality, fundamental DCF, macro/liquidity-driven expectations, and reflexivity — converge on C (Hold/Watch), refuse to buy at $14.50, and place the valuation hinge on one variable: whether economic EBIT margins and incremental returns on capital clear the ~9% cost of capital as share gains compound and the GAAP/adjusted earnings gap closes. We agree the franchise is genuinely dominant, the balance sheet is a fortress by industry standards, the servicing cash floor is real, the refi option is credible but dated (H1-2027), SBC is the per-share tax, the $31.5B goodwill+intangibles is an unproven bet, and governance is founder-controlled (Dan Gilbert ~62%). The biggest risks are a sustained high-rate tape (30-yr >7.4%), an impairment against negative tangible book (−$8.0B), a GAAP/SBC reckoning, and a share-gain stall. Therefore, at today's price the consensus is to hold and wait: accumulate in stages at $8–10, build at $6–8, buy with conviction below $6, and let demonstrated economic returns on capital — not the refi narrative — decide whether this dominant franchise becomes a wonderful investment.
